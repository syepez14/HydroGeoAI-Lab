Summary: This study evaluated the Snowmelt-Runoff Model (SRM) and the Génie Rural à X Paramètres Journalier (GRxJ) model family, analyzing the latter both independently and in combination with the CemaNeige snow module. SRM and GRxJ represent snowmelt-runoff and rainfall-runoff hydrological models, respectively.
