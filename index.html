About us

Remote sensing, artificial intelligence and aquatic systems

HydroGeoAI Lab develops interdisciplinary research combining remote sensing, geospatial technologies, artificial intelligence and environmental sciences to study aquatic and fluvial systems.

Remote Sensing

Hyperspectral imaging, optical remote sensing, UAV observations and advanced environmental sensing.

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning and deep learning approaches for environmental monitoring, prediction and geospatial analysis.

Aquatic & Fluvial Systems

Research on rivers, lakes, reservoirs, sediment dynamics, water quality and hydro-geomorphological processes.
