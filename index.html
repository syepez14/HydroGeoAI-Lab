About us

Remote sensing, artificial intelligence and aquatic systems

HydroGeoAI Lab develops interdisciplinary research combining advanced remote sensing, geospatial technologies, artificial intelligence and environmental sciences to understand aquatic, fluvial and terrestrial systems.

🛰️

Remote Sensing

Hyperspectral imaging, optical remote sensing, UAV observations, LiDAR, photogrammetry and advanced environmental sensing.

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, deep learning and computer vision for environmental monitoring, prediction, classification and geospatial analysis.

💧

Aquatic & Fluvial Systems

Research on rivers, lakes, reservoirs, sediment dynamics, hydrology, water quality and hydro-geomorphological processes.

Mission

Our mission is to advance the understanding, monitoring and sustainable management of aquatic and fluvial systems through the integration of remote sensing, geomatics, environmental sciences and artificial intelligence. We develop innovative methods that transform complex environmental data into useful scientific knowledge and decision-making tools.

Vision

Our vision is to become a multidisciplinary reference laboratory in Latin America for advanced environmental observation and artificial intelligence, connecting field measurements, remote sensing, three-dimensional information and predictive modeling to address emerging challenges in water resources, rivers, ecosystems and climate change.

Applied Capabilities

Services

HydroGeoAI Lab provides advanced remote sensing, geospatial analysis and artificial intelligence solutions for environmental applications.

🌿

Species Detection

Identification and classification of species using RGB, multispectral and hyperspectral imagery.

🔢

Individual Quantification

Automated detection and counting of individuals using computer vision and artificial intelligence.

🚁

UAV Remote Sensing

Drone-based surveys for environmental, forest and fluvial monitoring.

🌈

Hyperspectral Imaging

Hyperspectral acquisition and analysis for vegetation, water quality and environmental characterization.

📡

LiDAR & 3D Mapping

Point clouds, terrain reconstruction, vegetation structure and 3D environmental models.

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, deep learning and computer vision for automated environmental data analysis.
