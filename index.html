About us

Remote sensing, artificial intelligence and aquatic systems

HydroGeoAI Lab develops interdisciplinary research combining advanced remote sensing, geospatial technologies, artificial intelligence and environmental sciences to understand aquatic, fluvial and terrestrial systems.

🛰️

Remote Sensing

Hyperspectral imaging, optical remote sensing, UAV observations, LiDAR, photogrammetry and advanced environmental sensing.

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, deep learning and computer vision for environmental monitoring, prediction, classification and geospatial analysis.

💧

Aquatic & Fluvial Systems

Research on rivers, lakes, reservoirs, sediment dynamics, hydrology, water quality and hydro-geomorphological processes.

Mission

Advance the understanding, monitoring and sustainable management of aquatic and fluvial systems through remote sensing, geomatics, environmental sciences and artificial intelligence.

Vision

Become a multidisciplinary reference laboratory in Latin America for advanced environmental observation and artificial intelligence applied to water, rivers, ecosystems and climate challenges.
