Summary: PhD candidate focused on satellite remote sensing of inland waters, with particular emphasis on satellite altimetry and lake-level dynamics. His research integrates SWOT observations, multi-mission satellite altimetry processed using AlTiS, and multisensor optical and radar imagery to delineate water bodies, generate water masks, and reconstruct long-term time series of lake surface area, water levels, and water storage variations. His current work focuses on Andean volcanic lakes in southern Chile, investigating their hydrological responses to climate variability. His broader research interests also include lake hydrogeochemistry and lacustrine sediments.

Keywords: Satellite remote sensing; Satellite altimetry; Lake hydrology; SWOT; Volcanic lakes; Climate variability
