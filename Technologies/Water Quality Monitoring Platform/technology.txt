Summary: we developed an advanced monitoring system using remote sensing technology. Through satellite images, hyperspectral spectroscopy and bio-optical models, we were able to assess and monitor water quality in four strategic lakes in south-central Chile: Laguna Grande de San Pedro de la Paz, Lago Lanalhue, Lago Villarrica and Lago Llanquihue..
