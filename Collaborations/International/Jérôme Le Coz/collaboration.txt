Summary: My research focuses on River Hydraulics, Hydrometry, and Fluvial Morphodynamics. My work includes river discharge measurement, water and sediment transport, and uncertainty analysis. I develop and apply numerical models and image-based techniques to study river flows and monitor sediment dynamics. I also contribute to open-source tools for hydrometry and collaborate on field-based research and the improvement of river monitoring methods.
