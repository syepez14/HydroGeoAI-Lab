Summary: My research focuses on Remote Sensing, Ecohydrology, and Agrohydrology. My work combines satellite observations, GIS, and hydrological modelling to investigate climate change impacts on vegetation and water resources. I also study evapotranspiration, surface energy fluxes, drought monitoring, and watershed hydrology, with applications to agricultural water management and ecosystem sustainability.
