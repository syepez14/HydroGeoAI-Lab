Summary: My research/teaching focus is Environmental Hydraulics (EH). My past studies were on two-phases flows (reaeration, dropshaft, hydraulic jump), tidal bores and aquaculture fishnets. Then, I applied CFD methods to complex EH flows (water tanks, dead zones, hyporheic flows, anaerobic digesters, spillways). In the last decade I was involved in field-based studies in the Amazon, Congo, Orinoco, Volga, Yangtze, Yarlung Zangbo and Yellow River basins. I am also studying the history of water science.
