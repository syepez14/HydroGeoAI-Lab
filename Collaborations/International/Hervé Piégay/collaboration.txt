Summary: My research focuses on Fluvial Geomorphology and River Restoration. My work investigates river channel dynamics, sediment transport, riparian vegetation, and the impacts of human activities on river systems. I use field measurements, GIS, remote sensing, and historical data to understand river changes and support sustainable river management and restoration.
